The mission

Here’s the big picture before you dive into the rules.

Exaltemps Solidaire is a hackathon with a purpose. Our goal is to deliver real IT work to associations that don’t have any IT expertise.

We’ve picked 10 to 15 champions from our company, and that’s you. Each association needs a fully working AWS platform to host its application.

Your job is to build an autonomous platform powered by AI, one that needs as little human intervention as possible to keep running.

What you need to deliver

Five deliverables are expected. Tap each one to tick it off as you go.

The rules of the game

Follow these in order. They’re short, and they’ll save you hours.

Get your tools ready

Make sure you have Claude Code installed, plus VS Code or IntelliJ, whichever you prefer.

Clone the application code

Everyone on the team should have the application’s Git repository on their own machine. We’ll share the link with you later.

Start from the Terraform example

You can also clone our example Terraform repository. It already bootstraps the project, so you don’t need to lose time configuring Terraform and AWS from scratch. Skip the setup and spend your energy on the agents.

Split the work into two distinct tracks

Divide and conquer, or work on both together. Just make sure the two parts stay clearly separate.

Track one: the builder agent

Your agent calculates and builds the Terraform modules, then provisions the right AWS components for the application’s needs. You also build the harness around it.

SecurityFinOpsHigh availability

Track two: the self-maintaining agent

Your agent watches the platform’s health, reacts to outages, and sends notifications when needed.

Can’t fix it automatically? Then tell us clearly that a human needs to step in. Keep a human in the loop.

Don’t forget the tests

Test your Terraform, your agents and your harness. A platform that runs itself has to be one you can trust.

Ship every deliverable

Before you call it done, check that all five deliverables are ready: the Terraform repo, the AI agents, the app URL, the monitoring URL and the quickstart guide. Use the checklist above.

Build something autonomous. Build it for someone who needs it. Good luck, champions.
